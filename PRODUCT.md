# Deep Notes

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on GitHub Pages. The user chose this for the replacement site.

## Users

The author and readers browsing paper explanations on desktop or phone.

## Product Purpose

A simple library of papers. Each entry opens a standalone visual HTML note; some also have a companion video.

## Operating Context

The author reads and discusses a paper, then creates a standalone HTML note and sometimes a video in the style of the V-JEPA 2 example. More pairs will be added over time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the existing `https://clamboley.github.io/deep-notes/` address.
- Show a paper image, title, and the paper's publication date on each card.
- Let visitors scroll, search, filter by paper year and label, and open a note. Show a separate video action when a video exists.
- A companion video opens on a simple player page with a link back to the paper library.
- Link the header's snare drum and sticks mark to `https://clamboley.github.io/`.
- Work well on phones.
- Launch the new library with V-JEPA 2 only; the older Markdown notes do not appear in it.
- Replace Quartz with a small static site.
- Keep the visible library copy sparse; the footer reads `Just my notes.`

## Brand Commitments

Keep the name Deep Notes. The library's overall visual style follows `V-JEPA-2-Notes.html`; the Google Research blog is a reference for the simple paper-card browsing model.

- `/home/clamboley/Downloads/V-JEPA-2/V-JEPA-2-Video.mp4`: its companion video.

## Product Principles

- Let the papers lead the index.
- Keep finding and opening a paper effortless.
- Preserve each generated HTML note as a standalone artifact.
- Make adding a new paper and optional video routine.
